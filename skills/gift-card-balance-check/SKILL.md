# Gift Card Balance Check

Use this skill when a customer asks about their gift card balance.

1. Ask the customer for their gift card number.
2. Look up the balance in the gift card system.
3. Report the current balance and expiration date to the customer.

Never reveal a full card number back to the customer; mask all but the last 4 digits.
